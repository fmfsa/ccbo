% M14: Figure 5 caption, replace final sentence about curve endpoints.
At each cost, we report the mean and standard error only when every seed of
that method has a recorded incumbent at or below that cost and has reached
at least that cost. We use the latest recorded incumbent within the budget
and do not extrapolate beyond a run's terminal cost. Initialization and split
initialization are included.

% M14: replace Table 4 caption.
Initialization-inclusive cost accounting for the controlled and arm-based
family suites. Init, Logged and Total are mean costs across seeds, displayed
rounded to the nearest integer; split initialization is displayed separately
where applicable. $C^\ast=\min_{m,s}C_{\mathrm{final}}(m,s)$ is the smallest

$C^\ast$ uses each run's latest incumbent with cost at most $C^\ast$, with no
extrapolation. ``n/a'' denotes a method whose complete initial design exceeds
$C^\ast$. The CEO rows use the supplementary protocol described in Appendix~C.

Final objective values, mean$\pm$standard error over paired seeds, and paired
finite-budget differences with 95\% confidence intervals. The paired difference
is quotient minus base for minimization tasks and base minus quotient for
maximization tasks; negative values favor the quotient method. These empirical
finite-budget differences are not the theoretical price $\Delta(\Pi)$.

% M22: append to §7 limitations; protocol details are in the methods patch.
The controlled suite uses exact population responses, so it isolates graph
and action-space effects without intervention measurement noise. The family
experiments use their stated simulator and scoring protocols, including
stochastic PSA responses. These results do not establish robustness to
arbitrary observation noise, alternative cost models, or clinical deployment.
The PSA study is a simulator benchmark; the Coral benchmark uses the cited
fitted SCM.
